# What works for a Tehran gold page on Instagram (2026-10-27)

Short notes behind the plan. No Iran-specific data exists for jewellery pages; where advice is general it says so.

## Formats, each with its own job
- **Reels** reach people who don't follow the page yet. The first 3 seconds decide it: the piece close up, catching light, no intro. Original footage only (no watermark from other apps).
- **Carousels** earn saves and trust. Teaching posts (عیار، مثقال، واژه‌های بازار) are the kind people save and send; we end each with «ذخیره کنید» or «بفرستید».
- **Stories** keep followers coming back: polls, quizzes, question box, and the daily price board (people in Tehran check the price every day; a page that posts it gets opened daily).
- Single photos reach the fewest new people; we use them for shop pieces as 2–3 slide posts (photo, second photo, ivory detail card).

## Signals
- Sends (DM shares) and saves count far more than likes. Watch time counts most for Reels.
- Reply to comments and DMs in the first hour.

## Hashtags and words
- Instagram allows at most 5 hashtags; they categorise, they don't bring reach. The first words of the caption matter more (keywords: گوشواره طلا، گردنبند طلا، قیمت طلا، مثقال، عیار).
- Sets rotate by topic (pieces / teaching / history) and always end with the brand tag #لئون_گلد.
- No digits-with-ZWNJ tags; check each tag's page on Instagram before first use.

## Times (Tehran)
- A 2023 study of Iranian business pages found late morning (9–12) best and Thursday the best day ([IJWR](https://doaj.org/article/07edf5177c7a4808aca94ca7e4bce57f)). General guides add evenings ([zoomit](https://www.zoomit.ir/software-application/437025-best-times-to-post-on-instagram)).
- Plan: teaching carousels 11:00, shop pieces 21:00, price story ~11:30 Sat–Thu (after the market opens). Friday: stories only.
- After two weeks read Insights (when followers are online, reach from non-followers, saves, sends) and move the times.

## Guardrails
- No prices or weights in posts; «در دایرکت بپرسید», priced at the hour's union rate.
- No «اصل», «پرفروش», «تضمینی», no invented reviews or numbers.
- Museum images: CC0/public domain, credited, marked as not Leon Gold's work.
